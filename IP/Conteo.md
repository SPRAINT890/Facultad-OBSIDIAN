## Permutaciones
$p_n = n!$
### Reglas del factorial
7! = 7.6.5! = 7.6.5.4! = 7.6.5.4.3.2.1
1! = 1

## Arreglos
$C_3^5 . P_3 = A_3^5$
$A_m^n = m(m-1) ... (m-n+1)$
$A_3^5 = \frac{7!}{3!} = \frac{7!}{(7-4)!}$

## Las Tres Formulas
$A_n^m = \frac{m!}{(m-n)!}$
$P_n = n!$
$C_n^m = \frac{m!}{(m-n)!n!}$

## Arreglos con Repeticion
$AR_n^m = m^n$
